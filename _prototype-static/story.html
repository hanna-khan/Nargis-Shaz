Our story

Two names, one idea

Nargis & Shaz began at a kitchen table, with a simple frustration: beautiful clothes rarely understood how women actually live.

They move between boardrooms and family tables, from early flights to late dinners, from everyday errands to the biggest celebrations of the year. We design for all of it: clothing that is easy to wear, effortless to style, and made to be loved for a very long time.

Natural, always

Silk, cashmere, cotton and linen from mills we know by name. We avoid fabrics that will not age well.

Made by hand

Our embroidery and finishing are done by artisans in small ateliers, paid fairly and credited honestly.

Designed to last

Timeless shapes, generous seams and repairs for life. The best piece is the one you keep.
